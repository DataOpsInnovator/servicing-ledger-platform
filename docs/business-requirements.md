# Business requirements and acceptance criteria — v2

## Product charter

**Problem:** repeated or interrupted servicing writes can lead to duplicate entries, incomplete downstream processing and uncertainty in operational reconciliation. **Goal:** an internal, synthetic journal that supports consistent recording, corrective reversal, traceability and repeatable recovery. No funds movement, customer PII, interest computation or external financial settlement. A real business deployment requires independent product, financial control, legal and security approval.

## Assumptions

Single internal organization, stable global idempotency keys generated by the calling system; clients must not reuse keys across unrelated operations. Role-level access allows authorized staff to read all synthetic entries. Local shared tokens cannot identify people; cloud OIDC hashes issuer/subject for attribution. Operators can initiate reversals, but there is no maker-checker approval workflow. Correction consists of reversal plus new entry, separate transactions; there is no multi-entry atomic correction or partial reversal. Loan-level totals are **journal amounts**, not outstanding balances, and do not compare with an external servicing system. No interest, multi-currency, posting periods, chargebacks, ledger chart of accounts, or double-entry balancing.

## Proposed release gates

Business/control owner approves workflow and synthetic data; engineering owner confirms tests, migrations, scans and rollback strategy; security owner signs off identity and access boundaries, encryption, retention and egress; SRE validates alerts, failure recovery, PITR and workload performance. Production service objectives require measured evidence in representative infrastructure. No formal SOC 2, PCI DSS, GLBA or other compliance certification is claimed.
